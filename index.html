FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still curious? Email the team.

How does the 85% match accuracy work?

Wynt's AI reads both your CV and each job description, then scores the overlap across skills, experience level, industry, and keywords. 85%+ means you rank in the top decile of likely applicants.

What are tokens?

Tokens power AI actions — analyzing a CV, generating a tailored rewrite, or running an interview rehearsal. The free plan includes 65 tokens; Pro is unlimited.

Which job boards do you cover?

50+ boards including LinkedIn, Indeed, Bayt, Wuzzuf, Naukri, Wellfound, Y Combinator's Work at a Startup, and more — with strong MENA coverage.

Is my data private?

Your CV is encrypted at rest, never sold, and never used to train third-party models. You can delete your account and all data anytime.

Does it support Arabic?

Yes — Wynt fully supports Arabic CVs, job descriptions, and interviews, with RTL-aware analysis.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel your Pro subscription in one click. You'll keep access until the end of your billing period.
