FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still curious? Talk to the team.

Is Wynt really free?

Yes — CV scoring and matching are free forever. Premium coaching and unlimited rewrites are available on Wynt Pro.

How private is my data?

Your CV is encrypted at rest, never sold, and never used to train third-party models. You can delete your account anytime.

Which file formats are supported?

PDF, DOC, DOCX, and plain text. You can also paste your CV directly into Wynt.

Does Wynt work for non-tech roles?

Absolutely. Wynt supports roles across engineering, design, product, marketing, sales, operations, and more.

Can recruiters find me on Wynt?

Only if you opt in. You control who sees your profile and can switch to private mode anytime.
