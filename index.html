FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still curious? Email the team.

Is this really free?

Yes. Uploading your resume, getting your AI review, and seeing matched roles is 100% free for candidates.

Who sees my resume?

Only Wynt's AI processes it. Employers only see your profile if you choose to apply or opt into recruiter outreach.

Which file formats can I upload?

PDF, DOC, and DOCX up to 10MB. If your resume is in another format, convert it to PDF first.

How long does the review take?

Most reviews land in your inbox in under 5 minutes. Complex resumes may take slightly longer.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. One click from your account, or email us at privacy@wynt.ai. We honor deletion requests immediately.
